Turbine exhaust round 2, commit 3: TN D-3836 gas film (2026-09-25)
==================================================================

modified Hatch-Papell correlation [TN-D3836 p.8-9]) instead of the liquid nozzle-slot decay law.

(eta 0.74 at the exit): its uncooled extension wall goes 1512 -> 1221 K. The J-2's regen extension
barely moves; its jacket picks up a little less heat, so the regen Isp credit shifts ~0.1 s.
