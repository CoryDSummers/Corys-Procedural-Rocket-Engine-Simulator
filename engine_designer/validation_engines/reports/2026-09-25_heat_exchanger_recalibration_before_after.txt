Turbine exhaust round 2, commit 4: heat exchanger recalibration + helium coil (2026-09-25)
==========================================================================================
Changes:
- LOX_TO_GOX_DH_J_KG 4.0e5 -> 6.0e5 J/kg: real F-1 outlet temperature (90 K -> 516 K / 470 F)
  [F1-Man Fig 3-29], enthalpy via CoolProp at the F-1 ox pump discharge pressure (1,602 psia).
- New HE_HX_DH_J_KG = 5193*(397-63) ~ 1.73e6 J/kg (ideal monatomic cp): the F-1's SAME exhaust
  heat exchanger also carries a helium coil (fuel-tank pressurization), one shell [F1-Man
  Sec.1-71/1-72]. New field turbine_exhaust_hx_he_kgs (schema 10), allowed on ANY propellant
  pair (helium is a pressurant, not tied to the propellants); the LOX coil stays LOX-pair only.
  Both coils' duty adds in exhaust_stream().
- HX can sizing reverse-solved against the corpus F-1's own duct bore (24.3 in, itself close to
  the F-1's real 24 in turbine-exhaust-manifold-end diameter): HX_CAN_DIA_DUCT_MULT 2.5 -> 1.77,
  HX_CAN_LENGTH_DUCT_MULT 3.0 -> 2.39, giving the corpus F-1 a 43.0 x 58.1 in can vs the real
  43 x 58 in envelope. The can is now drawn TAPERED (new frustum_mesh primitive, 1.5x duct bore
  inlet -> 0.9x outlet, mesh-only; mass still uses the plain-cylinder characteristic diameter).
  Duty never sizes the can - only the duct bore does (flagged in ASSUMPTIONS).
- Corpus F-1 now carries real HX flows: 1.81 kg/s GOX + 0.27 kg/s He (4 + 0.6 lb/s [F1-Man Fig
  3-29]) - previously unset (no GOX flow in hand). Exhaust film temperature drops ~12 K
  (829.7 -> 817.8 K), which very slightly COOLS the extension (1221 -> 1217 K, since the film's
  own temperature enters the adiabatic-wall-under-film formula). The HX can itself adds ~238 kg
  to the corpus F-1's turbine-exhaust hardware (335 kg total vs 98 kg before - a real, previously
  absent structural mass at this scale) - out of a 23,635 kg dry mass (~1%).
- GUI: a second "He coil flow" slider next to the GOX one; turbopump diagram / cfg_writer notes
  list both coils when present.
A/B computed in ONE environment (a `git worktree` of the pre-commit-4 HEAD vs the working tree),
so no cross-machine ULP noise. Every OTHER design in the corpus shows only the new
turbine_exhaust_hx_he_kgs field appearing in its serialized inputs at its default (0.0) - a
schema-only diff, not a physics change; J-2 additionally echoes the new hx_he_kgs=0 key inside
its turbine_exhaust dict for the same reason. No other numbers moved.
