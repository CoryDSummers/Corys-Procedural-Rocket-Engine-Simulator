Turbine-exhaust handling (2026-09-24): overboard duct / aspirator / nozzle injection
====================================================================================
Before = HEAD c578135 (the GG flow-accounting fix), after = this change. BOTH were computed in
the same Python environment (python 3.12, numpy 2.4.6, scipy 1.17.1, cloud container) with a
git worktree of HEAD, so last-ULP drift vs golden/ does not show up. Full field diff below.

What changed physically for GG / tap-off engines (physics/turbine_exhaust.py):
- The flat GG_DUMP_ISP_FRACTION 0.55 / TAP_OFF 0.80 are RETIRED. Exhaust Isp is now the ideal
  expansion of the turbine's own exhaust gas through the chosen disposal mode, times
  EXHAUST_THRUST_EFFICIENCY 0.96 (F-1 pinned). The default overboard_duct is a plain sonic duct
  exit (k ~0.25-0.5 of chamber Isp).
- The turbine PR is min(cap, inlet / the outlet the exhaust's back pressure needs). Every
  corpus open-cycle engine sits at its cap (22 GG / 18 tap-off); only low-Pc sea-level boosters
  such as the H-1 (PR 17.7) are back-pressure limited.
- Open cycles now CLOSE ON THE TARGET THRUST. EngineDesign.compute rescales chamber flow until
  chamber + exhaust thrust (with every Isp adjustment) = target_vac_thrust_n. Chamber flow
  scales ~0.98-0.99 (reported as thrust_closure_scale). The previous commit had let open
  cycles sit above target by their exhaust thrust, which pushed J-2 and Merlin-1D out of their
  cited ±1% vacuum-thrust bands. With closure they are back on target.
- Corpus F-1: nozzle_injection at eps 10. The 8%-of-fuel liquid slot-film stand-in is removed
  and the turbine-exhaust gas film (~811 K) cools the extension instead. The extension wall
  goes 1430 K (stand-in) -> 1548 K (exhaust film; vs 1970 K with no film at all). Engine Isp
  goes up because the stand-in's dump penalty is gone.
- Corpus J-2: nozzle_injection at eps 10.9 (cat-eyes 10.45-11.40). The exhaust film over the
  regen tubes downstream of eps 10.9 cuts jacket heat (Q wall 57 -> ~49 MW).
- Cory's tap-off user designs (RS-29 / RS-29A / RS-29C / RS-30) lose ~4-5 s vac Isp. The flat
  0.80 was generous for their default sonic overboard duct (computed k ~0.33). Switching them
  to nozzle_injection or a shaped exhaust nozzle recovers part of it.
