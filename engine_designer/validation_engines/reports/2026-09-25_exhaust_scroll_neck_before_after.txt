Turbine-exhaust scroll manifold, commit 2: outlet neck + flame shield (2026-09-25)
================================================================================
Changes (physics/turbine_exhaust.py size_hardware, nozzle_injection only):
- The scroll now sits FORWARD of the injection station and OFF the nozzle wall, feeding aft-
  inboard through an annular NECK into the wall at the injection station, with a FLAME SHIELD on
  the wall beneath it - the F-1 manifold cutaway ([SP-8120 §2.2.5.3] text; figure reproduced on
  enginehistory.org RPE 8.12) and [F1-Man §1-18] ("welded to a flame shield ... welded to the
  thrust chamber outer wall"). Drawing-read constants (Tier 3), x the scroll's inlet tube radius
  r: torus centre NECK_AXIAL_OFFSET_TUBE_R_MULT 1.1 r forward, inner edge NECK_RADIAL_GAP_
  TUBE_R_MULT 0.35 r off the wall, neck NECK_WIDTH_TUBE_DIA_FRAC 0.25 x the tube dia high. The
  same construction stands in for the J-2's flat base ring.
- Mass: neck = two sheet walls (hoop-sized on the turbine outlet pressure over the half-height,
  floored at the 1 mm gauge), shield = one 1 mm sheet from the torus's forward tangent to the
  injection station; Haynes 230 as the rest of the exhaust hardware. Added to the hardware mass.
- Drawn as two revolved closed sections (the shield at a visible thickness floor, cosmetic).
- Injection station, gas-film slot and all performance unchanged (engine vac Isp identical).
A/B = the golden (commit 12447a0) vs the working tree, same environment. F-1 and J-2 move; every
other open-cycle design only gains turbine_exhaust_hardware.neck = None (schema-only).
