Turbine-exhaust scroll manifold, commit 1: tangential scroll + tangential duct entry (2026-09-25)
==============================================================================================
Changes:
- The nozzle_injection exhaust manifold is now a SCROLL (manifold.RING_KIND_SCROLL), like the
  real F-1 and J-2: the duct runs TANGENTIALLY into a one-way torus "of decreasing (from inlet to
  exit) cross-sectional area" [F1-Man §1-18] (photos: F-1 thrust chamber, enginehistory.org RPE
  8.12; J-2, Science Museum 1977-0402). It was a T-split header sized on HALF the flow per
  branch (inlet bore = duct bore / sqrt 2), tapering symmetrically at blend 0.5.
- Sized on the FULL exhaust flow at its inlet (inlet bore = the duct bore), constant-velocity
  taper (EXHAUST_SCROLL_TAPER_BLEND 1.0 - Cory's call), floored at MANIFOLD_TAPER_MIN_AREA_
  FRACTION 0.15 (reached at ~306 deg). The scroll turns so its inlet sits wherever its duct lands.
- plumbing.PlumbingRun.root_mode ("auto" | "surface" | "tangential", schema 15): a
  turbine_exhaust run leaves a scroll tangentially from its inlet face - no root reducer, and
  SCROLL_ENTRY_K = 0 (no Borda-Carnot split dump; Tier 3). The default duct seed for a scroll is
  the F-1 photo's route: tangent leg out to the port's radius, an elbow toward the turbine's
  station, then the auto legs close onto the port. A pre-15 baked exhaust run is migrated to
  root_mode "surface" (its radial T kept, warn-only advisory).
- Validate turbine-exhaust row (k): corpus F-1 inlet bore 24.3 in vs the real 24 in heat-
  exchanger manifold end [F1-Man §1-72] (+1.3 %).
A/B = the golden (HEAD 7c2bd6a, verified bit-identical before the change) vs the working tree,
same environment. Only F-1 and J-2 (the two nozzle_injection designs) move numerically; every
other open-cycle design only gains the new keys (implicit_run root_mode, plumbing_results
root_tangential / root_reducer) - a schema-only diff. Performance (Isp, film, back pressure) is
unchanged: the default duct's loss is reported only (the lumped EXHAUST_INJECTION_PRESSURE_RATIO
stays in charge), and the ring geometry does not enter the gas film.

Reading it: the F-1's scroll inlet now matches its real 24 in (the old split header's 17.2 in
was the half-flow bore); the scroll narrows to 9.4 in at its tail. The ring is ~10 % heavier
(bigger section) but the tangential duct is shorter and lighter (no radial standoff + U-turn),
so dry mass falls ~50 kg (F-1) / ~10 kg (J-2). The reported duct loss halves or better: no
ring-entry dump and no root reducer.
