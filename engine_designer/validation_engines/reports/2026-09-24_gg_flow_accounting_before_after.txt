GG / tap-off flow & thrust accounting fix (2026-09-24)
======================================================
Before = HEAD e43e713, after = this change. BOTH were computed in the same Python environment
(python 3.12, numpy 2.4.6, scipy 1.17.1, cloud container) with a git worktree of HEAD, so
last-ULP library drift vs golden/ does not show up as a change. Every field that differs is
listed below (the full _diff, with no limit).

**The bug.**
- cycles.gas_generator_result reports gg_flow_fraction = mdot_gg / mdot_CHAMBER.
- turbopump.engine_isp_with_gg_dump blended it as if it were a share of TOTAL flow.
- design thrust = chamber mdot x that engine Isp.

As a result, GG / tap-off thrust came out about x*(1-k) BELOW the level the chamber actually
produces, and mdot_kgs left out the GG / tap-off draw.

**The fix.**
- mdot_kgs = chamber + GG / tap-off flow. The new mdot_chamber_kgs carries the chamber alone.
- Engine Isp = (mdot_c*Isp_c + mdot_gg*k*Isp_c) / mdot_total.
- thrust = mdot_total x engine Isp.
- The dump and slot-film bleed penalties are now fractions of total flow.
- A new cycle_result["gg_fraction_of_total"] is reported. gg_flow_fraction keeps its
  chamber-relative meaning.
- Unchanged: GG_DUMP_ISP_FRACTION is still 0.55 in this commit, so the diff shows only the
  accounting change.

**Also fixed by the same change, affecting staged cycles (RS-25, RD-180, Raptor-2).**
- The regen Isp credit's "chamber share" was 1 - cycle_result["gg_flow_fraction"].
- Staged results fill that field with the PREBURNER fraction, so the credit was withheld from
  preburner flow. That flow does go through the chamber.
- The share is now mdot_chamber / mdot_total, which is 1.0 for staged cycles. Their Isp and
  thrust rise by 0.2-0.9 %.

**Target-thrust semantics are unchanged.** target_vac_thrust_n sets the CHAMBER flow, and
every Isp adjustment (regen credit, dump, film, now GG exhaust) moves the thrust from there.
Open-cycle engines therefore now sit above target by the exhaust's own thrust, instead of
below it.
