# Assumptions and magic numbers

A working reference for "how much of this tool is made up" - every constant that
ISN'T derived from first principles, organized by how much you should trust it.
If you want to tighten something up, this tells you exactly which file/constant
to go change and what it would take to make it less of an estimate.

## Tier 1 - Validated (not magic)

- **The isentropic gas-dynamics equations themselves** (`physics/isentropic.py`):
  area-Mach relation, thrust coefficient, characteristic velocity formula. Exact
  textbook relations (Sutton & Biblarz, *Rocket Propulsion Elements*), verified by
  the module's own self-checks (round-trips, monotonicity) - not estimates at all.
- **Bipropellant performance on the equilibrium tables** (P1 re-anchor, 2026-09-25;
  `combustion.performance_state` / `cf_vac_ideal` / `exit_pressure_ratio`,
  `thermo_tables.isp_vac_ideal_s` / `pe_over_pc_at_eps`): Tc, M, frozen chamber gamma,
  ideal shifting-equilibrium c*, ideal vacuum Isp and exit pressure at area ratios
  2-250 come from the same Cantera tables as the heat-transfer path (Sutton 5-5 gated),
  at the actual (MR, Pc) - so Isp now carries its real Pc dependence. The IDEAL is
  exact to the tables' accuracy; what is calibrated is on top: `DEFAULT_ETA_CSTAR`
  (Tier 2, cited) and `ETA_CF` (Tier 2, reverse-solved) - see those rows. The spot
  checks (`validate/performance.py` `run()` / `run_integration_checks()`: RD-111,
  RL10A-3-3, Aestus, AJ10-137, Raptor-2, + the MR-80B / Sprite monopropellants) are
  exact on their anchors by construction; the per-engine RESIDUALS of the other corpus
  engines are the honest error bar (`validation_engines/reports/2026-09-25_performance_
  reanchor_before_after.txt`: RS-25 -1.8 %, RD-180 -3.7 %, Merlin-1D -4.8 %,
  Rutherford -4.3 %, J-2 +1.4 %, Vulcain +0.9 %, RL10 corpus +2.4 %, F-1 +0.2 %).
  Re-run `python3 -m engine_designer.physics.validate` any time you touch combustion.py
  or the nozzle/injector wiring - this is the guardrail that caught the nozzle-
  efficiency double-counting bug. The old hand `_TABLES` (Tc/gamma/M per MR, no Pc,
  "effective" LOX/LH2 gamma/M - COOLING_AUDIT P1) now serves only the monopropellants.
- **`is_monopropellant`/the `Hydrazine` single-point table** (`physics/combustion.py`):
  the degenerate-table trick (`np.interp` against a length-1 array always returns
  that one value) is exact, not an approximation - it's what lets monopropellant
  reuse the whole pipeline without a parallel code path. The underlying Tc/gamma/M
  values themselves are Tier 2 (see below), same as any other pair's table.
- **Rao bell contour self-consistency** (`physics/nozzle_shapes.py`): the
  round-trip/monotonicity/tangent-line checks in its `__main__` are exact algebra
  checks, not estimates - but see Tier 2 for the underlying angle table itself.
- **`static_temperature_ratio`** (`physics/isentropic.py`): the exact isentropic
  T/Tc relation used to find the local gas temperature at the bell's cooling-
  transition point. Not an estimate - same status as the rest of the module.
- **`geometry3d.revolve_profile`**: exact algebra (every revolved point is
  checked to sit at exactly the source radius from the axis) - the 3D preview
  is a rendering of already-computed geometry, not new physics.
- **Combustion-gas heat-transfer properties** (2026-09-23 cooling audit,
  `combustion.heat_transfer_gas_properties` + `physics/thermo_tables.py`):
  for every bipropellant pair, Tc, FROZEN cp / gamma, mixture-averaged
  viscosity + frozen conductivity and the frozen Prandtl number at the actual
  (MR, Pc) come from a real chemical-equilibrium solve (Cantera, NASA-Glenn
  thermo, CEA liquid-reactant enthalpies, GRI-Mech transport), generated by the
  KEPT script `tools/property_tables/generate_property_tables.py` and self-
  checked against Sutton Table 5-5 (Tc/c*/M/Isp within ~2 %; two table errata
  documented in the script). They REPLACE `prandtl(gamma)` (Eucken, ~0.84 vs the
  real frozen ~0.6), `mixture_cp_j_kgk` and the Bartz viscosity fit on the
  heat-transfer path - fed the performance table's effective LOX/LH2 gamma/M,
  those made h_g swing ~1.7x across MR. `prandtl` / `mixture_cp_j_kgk` remain
  only for the monopropellants (no table; flagged `gas_property_source=legacy`)
  and the tap-off drive-gas cp. Coolant properties likewise: CoolProp tables
  (para-H2, CH4, n-dodecane as a flagged RP-1 SURROGATE) via
  `cooling.CoolantModel`, with an enthalpy (not cp*dT) march.
- **`combustion_stability.acoustic_modes`** (`physics/combustion_stability.py`):
  the chamber acoustic-mode frequency formulas `1L = a_e/(2Lc)`, `1T =
  0.59*a_e/dc`, `1R = 1.22*a_e/dc` `[Huzel Fig 4-59]` and `a_e = sqrt(gamma*R'*
  Tc/M)`. Textbook relations, not estimates; spot-checked against Vulcain HM-60's
  published T1 = 2424 Hz in `validate.py`'s `run_combustion_stability_check()`.
  Whether a given design's 1T lands in the "needs baffles/cavities" band is the
  only judgment call, and it only WARNS.
- **`combustion_stability.AID_DAMPING_CEILING_HZ` (4000) and
  `baffle_compartments_ok` (odd, >= 3)** (`physics/combustion_stability.py`):
  `[Sutton 9.3]` - injector-face baffles are designed to suppress modes below
  ~4000 Hz, and their compartment count is "always ODD" (an even count sits on
  the tangential-mode nodal lines and *enhances* the mode). Cited rules, not
  estimates. `STIFF_INJECTOR_DP_OVER_PC` (stiff 0.30 / very_stiff 0.42) is Tier
  2 - `[claude_lit/topics/05]` says a stability-driven injector runs dP/Pc up to
  ~0.35 (large) / ~0.5 (small) vs the ~0.15-0.25 nominal band; the two exact
  values are a choice within that range. The stiffer build's cost (higher pump
  discharge pressure -> turbine work -> GG bleed) then falls straight out of the
  existing feed-pressure chain, not a separate penalty.
- **`combustion.L_STAR_DEFAULT_M`** (`physics/combustion.py`): the per-pair
  characteristic-length values (LOX/RP-1 1.10, LOX/LH2 0.75, storables 0.85,
  Hydrazine 1.00) are read straight off `[Huzel Table 4-1]`. Only used to seed
  the GUI L* slider on pair-select and the residence-time method's auto default;
  `EngineDesign.lstar_m` keeps its flat 1.0 m default so no spot check moves.
- **`combustion.residence_time_from_lstar_s`** (`physics/combustion.py`):
  `t = L* * M * c* / (R_u * Tc)`, the exact algebraic inverse of `L* = Vc/At`
  given `Vc = t*mdot/rho_c`, `rho_c = Pc*M/(R_u*Tc)`, `At = mdot*c*/Pc`. So the
  `chamber_sizing_method="residence_time"` path in `geometry.chamber_geometry`
  is the same knob as L* in different units, not a new estimate - `validate.py`
  C4 pins the two to bit-equality at each pair's L* default.
- **Stay-time band 1-40 ms** (`design.py` C2): `ts = Vc*rho_gas/mdot`,
  `[Sutton eq. 8-10]`'s cited range. **Throat fillet radii** (`geometry.py` C3):
  R ~ 1.5*Rt convergent side, ~0.382*Rt downstream - the Rao/`[Huzel Sample 4-2]`
  values the sharp-cornered contour skipped before; the bell contour now carries
  the same ~0.382*Rt downstream arc (`nozzle_shapes.bell_profile_points`,
  `throat_fillet=True`). **`chamber_wall_fillet_r_over_rt`** (`geometry.py` C5):
  an R = (this)*Rt arc rounding the cylinder->convergent-cone corner; its removed
  gas volume is subtracted from the convergent volume so `L**At = Vc_total` stays
  exact. Default 0.0 (historical sharp corner, bit-identical). Tier 3 schematic
  contour refinement, same status as the throat fillet - verified to move the
  cooled wetted area <0.1% and `run_cooling_heat_flux_check` stays green.
  **`convergent_half_angle_deg` 20-45 deg** warn band is `[Huzel 4.3]`. All
  warn-not-block.
- **`cooling.bartz_hg`** SI form of `[Huzel eq. 4-13]`, the recovery temperature
  `T_aw = T_s + r (T0 - T_s)` with `r = Pr^(1/3)` per station
  (`cooling.adiabatic_wall_temperature_profile`, `[Huzel 4.4]` - it WAS `0.9*Tc`
  everywhere until the 2026-09-23 audit, ~350 K low at the throat), the full Bartz
  `sigma(T_wg/T0, M)` (`cooling.bartz_sigma`, `[Huzel eq. 4-14]`, omega 0.6 -
  iterated against the solved wall; was a constant 1.0), `cooling.radiative_wall_
  temperature` (`[Huzel eq. 4-38]`) and the series-resistance wall balance: the
  equations are exact. They are all combined in ONE per-station solve,
  `cooling/thermal_solve.solve_thermal` (see `engine_designer/COOLING_AUDIT.md`).
  With them the flux needs NO calibration: raw Bartz meets the cited J-2
  (28-57 MW/m2) and SSME (118 MW/m2 design point) throat fluxes [Wieseneck-J2].
  `BARTZ_RC_OVER_DT = 1.0` stays (within ~3 %).

The warning's advice is keyed to the sizing limit: floor = thermal-limited (higher-k liner / less flux), cap = pressure-limited (more/narrower tubes, less jacket pressure, stronger alloy), neither = no thickness carries both loads in that material. **`coax_shell` fails this check on almost any design, even near-throat-only cooling** - a large-radius thin shell inherently has a high r/t ratio, so nearly any net pressure differential produces high stress; this was confirmed to be a real, expected structural limitation (not a remaining bug) matching why real coax-shell construction only ever appeared on small/early engines (V-2, early Atlas - per `cooling.WALL_CONSTRUCTIONS`'s own comment), never scaled up - the warning text for `coax_shell` specifically says so rather than using the generic "worth a closer look" framing, and recommends `tube_wall`/`milled_channel` instead for larger chambers. `milled_channel` keeps its existing Tier-3 status and caveats unchanged (see prior entry text, now specific to this construction only) - still no real-engine spot check for it. Pre-existing gap this round does NOT fix, for any construction: `body_wall_thickness_m`/`ext_wall_thickness_m` (the RENDERED/MASS wall thickness elsewhere in `compute()`) never branch on `wall_construction` either - all three constructions still share one generic full-radius-derived thickness there, independent of the jacket-overpressure check's own now-construction-specific thickness handling. **2026-09-17 UPDATE - interfaced with the manifold/bypass system**: the check's own `jacket_pressure_pa` no longer recomputes `pc_feed + dp_injector_fuel + jacket_dp_pa` independently - it now reuses the already-computed, topology-aware `jacket_inlet_pressure_pa`/`jacket_return_pressure_pa` (`design.py:1290-1292`, shared with `manifold.size_jacket_manifolds`'s own ring sizing). `single_pass_countercurrent` (default) is unaffected (algebraically identical formula). Under `f1_split_reverse_flow`, the check now correctly uses `jacket_return_pressure_pa` (lower than the old unconditional full-inlet-pressure assumption) at the worst/aft station - resolving the "somewhat conservative" gap the row above used to flag. Combined with that row's real bypass-flow wiring, the check's reported combined stress now measurably responds to `manifold_bypass_fraction` under split-flow topology - verified end-to-end to DECREASE as bypass fraction increases (e.g. ~84 MPa at bypass=0 down to ~67 MPa at bypass=0.6 on a `tube_wall` test case), but via a non-obvious coupled mechanism: `jacket_dp_pa` itself INCREASES with bypass (smaller channels at reduced flow -> more friction loss, see the row above), yet the smaller channels also mean a smaller derived tube radius (Round 1's tube-radius-sized thickness) -> a thinner wall -> a lower thermal-restraint term, which dominates and outweighs the higher hoop term. (2026-09-23: with min-stress sizing the same direction holds - ~84 MPa at bypass=0 to ~64 MPa at 0.6 - via a smaller tube radius lowering the achievable `2*sqrt(dP*r*K)` minimum.) Trust this DIRECTION (verified in testing), not that the mechanism is the simple "less flow, less friction, less stress" one might naively assume |

## What this means in practice

- Trust the **Isp/thrust numbers** for the three built-in propellant pairs the most -
  they're the only thing spot-checked against real engines end-to-end.
- Trust the **qualitative behavior** of cycle/injector/material/nozzle choices
  (which direction things move, and why) more than the **exact magnitude** of any
  single non-propellant constant.
- The **expander feasibility check** is the newest and least-calibrated piece -
  treat a computed "feasible" margin for anything other than LOX/LH2 with real
  skepticism (the tool itself says so via a standing warning), and treat an
  "infeasible" result as directionally meaningful even if the exact margin number
  isn't.
- If you want to tighten a specific number, this doc tells you which file and
  constant to touch, and (via `physics/validate.py`) how the propellant-pair ones
  are kept honest so a future change can be checked the same way.
